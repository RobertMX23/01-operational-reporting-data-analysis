# Dashboard Notes

## Reporting Purpose

The dashboard will support operational and commercial monitoring of a women's shoes product catalog.

Its purpose is to help internal teams understand the structure, completeness and consistency of product catalog data.

---

## Main Reporting Areas

The dashboard should help monitor:

- Product catalog completeness
- Missing product attributes
- Brand distribution
- Category distribution
- Product attribute consistency
- Catalog reporting quality

---

## Data Quality Focus

Based on the dataset review, the reporting workflow should pay attention to:

- Null values
- Inconsistent categories
- Missing colors
- Missing count values
- Complex descriptions

---

## Intended Users

This type of reporting can support:

- Operations teams
- Commercial teams
- Customer support teams
- Management teams

---
