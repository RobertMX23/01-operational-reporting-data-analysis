# Operational Reporting Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project simulates an operational data reporting workflow for a digital business.

The goal is to transform raw operational data into clean, reliable and useful reports for internal business teams.

---

## Business Problem

Digital businesses need accurate and updated operational reports to monitor performance, identify issues and support decision-making.

However, raw operational data often contains inconsistencies, missing values, duplicated records or formats that make reporting unreliable.

---

## Why It Matters

Reliable reports help internal teams track KPIs, detect operational problems and make better decisions.

This type of analysis is especially useful for operations, commercial, customer support and management teams.

---

## Solution Approach

The project follows a structured analytics workflow:

1. Understand the business context
2. Review and clean the dataset
3. Validate data quality
4. Use SQL queries to answer business questions
5. Create operational KPIs
6. Build reporting outputs
7. Document the process and findings
