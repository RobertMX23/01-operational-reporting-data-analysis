# Executive Summary

## Project Context

This project focuses on an operational and commercial reporting case using a women's shoes product catalog dataset.

The dataset represents product catalog information that can be used to analyze brands, categories, colors, dates, descriptions and product data quality.

---

## Business Problem

Digital businesses need accurate and updated product catalog reports to monitor performance, identify data quality issues and support internal decision-making.

However, product catalog data can contain null values, inconsistent categories, missing attributes and complex descriptions that make reporting less reliable.

---

## Why It Matters

Reliable product catalog reporting helps internal teams understand the structure and quality of their product data.

This type of analysis supports operational, commercial, customer support and management teams by improving visibility into catalog completeness, product attributes and reporting consistency.

---

## Analytical Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw product catalog data into a cleaner and more structured reporting workflow.

The analysis will focus on:

- Data cleaning and validation
- Product catalog quality
- Brand and category reporting
- Operational KPIs
- SQL-based business questions
- Dashboard documentation
- Business insights and recommendations

---

## Expected Business Value

At the end of the project, the analysis should help answer questions such as:

- What product catalog fields require cleaning or validation?
- Which attributes have missing or inconsistent information?
- How can product catalog data be structured for reporting?
- What KPIs can help monitor product catalog quality?
- What information would help internal teams make better decisions?
